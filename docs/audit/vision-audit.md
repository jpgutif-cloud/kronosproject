# KRONOS — Product-Vision Audit
> Generated 2026-06-30 via PCOS (`pcos_run_audit` on `src/` + manual review of vision docs).
> Status of this doc: analysis only. Reconciliation decisions live in `docs/decisions/0001-vision-reconciliation.md`.

## 1. What KRONOS claims to be

An autonomous multi-agent system (ARLO + REX/ARLO-analyst/ZARA/PIP) that builds a service
business from a small seed toward ~$7,000 USD — enough to buy a top-tier MacBook Pro for
local LLM inference — while **livestreaming every decision** so owners/viewers can watch the
agent team work. Framed as "AI building toward its own infrastructure, in public."

## 2. Central finding — the autonomy contradiction

The **stated goal** ("fully autonomous, zero human intervention, legally earns the money")
contradicts the project's **own constitution**:

- `docs/constitution.md §5`: *"**Not fully autonomous.** Human oversight is a design
  requirement, not a limitation to be removed."*
- `§5`: *"Clients interact with a human operator, not directly with the AI… a human delivers
  the relationship."*
- `P6`: *"Human Retains Override Authority… `REQUIRE_APPROVAL=true` is the default."*
- Decision table: spend > $50, acquisitions, phase changes → **operator approval**.

The specs correctly encode a **semi-autonomous** design: the AI researches / drafts / scores /
proposes 24/7; a **human is the legal & financial principal** who approves sends, signs
contracts, and receives money. The vision and the specs currently describe two different
products. → resolved in `docs/decisions/0001`.

## 3. Real limitations (why "AI earns $7k with zero humans" is not buildable today)

- An AI cannot own a bank account, be a party to a contract, pass Stripe KYC, or apply to
  grants (CORFO / Anthropic Fellows require a person or legal entity). **100% of money handling
  requires a human principal.**
- The "sponsor donates the Mac" path is also human intervention (a human decides; a human
  receives the hardware).
- `gate()` blocks every TRANSACT / PUBLISH / COMMUNICATE action by design → **today KRONOS
  earns $0**. It is a well-architected skeleton + simulation, not a live earner.
- Realistic autonomy ceiling: **autonomous knowledge-work + human principal**.
